TriOfficial

Duties, checklists and rules for triathlon race officials

SupportPrivacy policy

Choose your position – Technical Delegate, Head Referee, Competition Jury or any Technical Official role – and the competition. TriOfficial shows what you have to do before, during and after the event, the key numbers for that race, and the rules and penalties that apply.

  • Tickable pre-, during- and post-event checklist with notes
  • Key facts: drafting, penalty times, wetsuit limits, swim and heat limits, protest deadlines
  • Rules and penalties filtered to your event and role
  • Export to spreadsheet, PDF, CSV or JSON
  • Works offline. No account, no tracking.

TriOfficial is an independent reference tool, not affiliated with or endorsed by World Triathlon. The official Competition Rules always prevail.
