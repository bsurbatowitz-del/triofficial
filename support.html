TriOfficial – triathlon race officials assistant

Getting started

  1. Event tab: choose your position and the competition (multisport, category, format, distance, level). Add the event name, date and venue.
  2. Checklist: tap the circle to tick a duty, tap the row for details and to add a note. Swipe right to tick quickly. Filter by phase or search.
  3. Key facts: the numbers for this race – distances, drafting, penalties, wetsuit and swim limits, heat, protest deadlines.
  4. Rules: rules that apply to the event (switch “Only rules for my position” off to see all).
  5. More: penalties, equipment to pack, notes, saved events and exports.

Saving and moving events

Everything saves automatically. Use “Save as event…” to keep several events. More › Export › “Event profile file” creates a .triof.json file that also opens in the TriOfficial desktop app for Ubuntu (File › Open profile), and desktop files can be imported with More › Import profile file.

Something wrong or missing?

Email b.surbatowitz@icloud.com with your position, the competition and the duty or rule concerned.
