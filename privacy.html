Privacy policy

TriOfficial for iPhone and Ubuntu · effective 17 October 2026

TriOfficial does not collect any personal data.

  • There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The app does not send anything over the internet. All reference content is included in the app.
  • Your choices, ticked duties, notes and saved events are stored only on your device (in the app's own storage, visible to you in the Files app).
  • When you export a report or profile, the file is shared only where you choose to send it (for example Mail or Files).
  • Deleting the app deletes all its data from your device.

Apple may provide the developer with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics if you have agreed to share them with app developers in your iPhone settings. These do not identify you.
